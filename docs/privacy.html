Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Afterimage doesn't collect, store, or share any of your data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and it doesn't connect to the internet.

Your camera

Afterimage uses your webcam for two things: passing your live video to the Afterimage camera, and recording your loop when you choose Record Loop. Video is processed only on your Mac. Afterimage only turns your webcam on while a video app is using the Afterimage camera, or while you're recording.

Your loop

Your recorded loop is saved as a single video file inside Afterimage's own folder on your Mac. Each new recording replaces the previous one. It never leaves your Mac unless you share it yourself. To delete it, choose Show Loop in Finder from the Afterimage menu and move the file to the Trash.

Video apps

When you choose Afterimage as your camera in Zoom, Meet, or another app, that app receives the video just as it would from a regular webcam. What it does with that video is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new date.
