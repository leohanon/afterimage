Support

Setting up Afterimage and fixing common problems.

Setting up

  1. Move Afterimage to your Applications folder and open it. Its icon appears in the menu bar.
  2. When asked, approve the camera in System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions: click ⓘ next to Camera Extensions and turn on Afterimage. Afterimage opens this page for you.
  3. Allow Afterimage to use your webcam when macOS asks.
  4. Choose Record Loop from the menu bar, then pick Afterimage as the camera in your video app. Apps that were already open when you installed Afterimage won't see it until you quit them completely (for Zoom: zoom.us → Quit Zoom) and reopen them.

Tip: For the most convincing loop, record a new one right before each call, so your lighting, clothes, and background match.

Troubleshooting

Afterimage isn't in my video app's camera list

Most often the video app was already open when Afterimage was installed. Video apps only look for cameras when they start, so quit the app completely (closing its window isn't enough; for Zoom, choose zoom.us → Quit Zoom) and open it again.

Still missing? Open Settings… from the Afterimage menu. If the Camera row asks you to approve or turn on the camera, click its button and switch Afterimage on. Then quit and reopen your video app again.

The camera shows a gray screen that says “Afterimage”

That means the Afterimage app isn't sending video. Make sure it's running (its icon is in the menu bar). If it is, quit it and open it again.

The webcam picture is black or missing in Live mode

Afterimage needs permission to use your webcam. Open Settings… from the Afterimage menu and click Allow Access…, or go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera. You can also choose a different webcam there.

Removing Afterimage

Quit Afterimage and drag it from Applications to the Trash. If the Afterimage camera still shows up in apps afterward, restart your Mac.

Still stuck?

Report a problem or ask a question on GitHub Issues. Please include your macOS version and which video app you're using.
